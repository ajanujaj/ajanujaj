# Hi, I'm Anuj 👋

I'm a **Machine Learning & AI Engineer** who enjoys taking models from a notebook to production. I've spent 5+ years in software and AI, working across classical ML, deep learning, computer vision and NLP. I hold an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Leeds**.

I care about the full lifecycle: understanding the data, choosing the right model, evaluating it honestly, and shipping it as a reliable system.

---

### 🧠 Areas I work in

- **Machine learning**: classification, clustering, segmentation, churn prediction, recommendation systems
- **Deep learning & computer vision**: CNNs, GNNs, object detection (YOLO), GANs, image classification and captioning
- **NLP & language models**: transformers, retrieval-augmented generation, speech and voice interfaces
- **ML engineering**: model serving with FastAPI, cloud deployment (AWS, GCP), evaluation and monitoring
- **Data**: analysis, feature engineering and visualisation with Pandas, NumPy, SQL and Seaborn
